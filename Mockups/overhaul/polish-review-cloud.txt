WHAT THE OWNER AND THE LAST REVIEWER ALREADY SAW (confirm or correct, then go further)
- Icons render too small (about 8-10 px where the mockup uses about 16 px): search magnifier, Columns, sidebar icons.
- Type sits off the scale: chips, sidebar items and buttons look a size smaller and lighter than the mockup; headings are close.
- Spacing is uneven: chips are shorter with tighter padding, the search box is thin, the empty selection bar is a tall box instead of a slim bar, gaps between groups differ per screen.
- The All Clients grid crowds its columns (names truncate) and shows password columns in clear; the mockup leaves those columns out by default.
- Colour accents (active sidebar marker, switch on-state) are not carried through consistently.
The suspected root cause: each package was built in a fresh session judged by tests, and nobody compared pixels with the mockup. Most fixes should therefore land in tokens.py and kit.py once, not per screen.
